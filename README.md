# Profit-and-Loss-Statement-AtliQ-Hardwares
# 📊AtliQ Hardwares – Profit & Loss Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the **Profit & Loss (P&L) performance of AtliQ Hardwares** for the fiscal years **2019, 2020, and 2021**.
The report helps us understand how the company's **sales, cost, and gross profit** changed over the years.
All values in the report are shown in **USD**.

## 🎯 Project Objective
The main objectives of this analysis are:
- Understand yearly Net Sales.
- Analyze Cost of Goods Sold.
- Analyze Gross Margin.
- Compare 2021 performance with 2020.
- Understand changes in Gross Margin %.

## 📊 Key Metrics
| Metric | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2021 vs 2020 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Net Sales | $87.5M | $196.7M | $598.9M | 204.5% |
| Cost of Goods Sold | $51.2M | $123.4M | $380.7M | 208.6% |
| Gross Margin | $36.2M | $73.3M | $218.2M | 197.6% |
| Gross Margin % | 41.4% | 37.3% | 36.4% | -2.3% |

---
## 🔍 Key Findings

### 1. Net Sales Increased
Net Sales increased from:
**$87.5M in 2019 → $196.7M in 2020 → $598.9M in 2021**
The report shows a **204.5% increase in 2021 compared with 2020**.
### 2. Cost of Goods Sold Also Increased
Cost of Goods Sold increased from:
**$51.2M in 2019 → $123.4M in 2020 → $380.7M in 2021**
The report shows a **208.6% increase in 2021 compared with 2020**.

### 3. Gross Margin Increased
Gross Margin increased from:
**$36.2M in 2019 → $73.3M in 2020 → $218.2M in 2021**
The report shows a **197.6% increase in 2021 compared with 2020**.
### 4. Gross Margin % Decreased
Gross Margin % changed from:
**41.4% in 2019 → 37.3% in 2020 → 36.4% in 2021**
The report shows a **2.3% decrease in 2021 vs 2020**.
---

## 💡 Simple Business Takeaway
The company recorded a strong increase in **Net Sales and Gross Margin** from 2020 to 2021.
However, **Gross Margin % decreased from 37.3% to 36.4%**, while Cost of Goods Sold increased by a higher percentage than Gross Margin.
This shows that sales and gross profit increased, while the gross margin percentage became slightly lower.
